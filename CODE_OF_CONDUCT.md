# Community code of conduct

We want participation in BB Graph Assistant to be welcoming and constructive. Treat people with respect, discuss technical ideas in good faith, and give feedback about work rather than personal traits.

Harassment, threats, discriminatory language, doxxing, and deliberate disclosure of another person's private data are not acceptable. Maintainers may edit or remove contributions that violate these expectations and may restrict participation when needed to protect the community.

Report a concern privately through the repository's GitHub contact or private reporting features. Please do not post another person's private information in a public issue.
